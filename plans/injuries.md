# Injuries & niggles

Not a diagnosis. Anything not settling in 2–3 weeks, or any red flag below → see a doctor or
physio. Daily scores live in `data/niggles.csv`.

## Traffic-light rule (applies to every session)
| Pain during / after (0–10) | Next morning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | back to baseline | 🟢 Train as planned |
| 4–5, or worse next morning | not settled | 🟡 Reduce: swap to low-impact (bike, swim, walk), shorten, flatter route |
| 6+, limping, or getting worse each session | — | 🔴 Stop that activity; change the plan; consider a physio |

**Red flags (stop, get it checked):** swelling, numbness/tingling, sharp pinpoint bone pain,
pain at rest or at night, chest pain, dizziness or fainting.
