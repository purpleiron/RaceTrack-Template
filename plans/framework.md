# Framework – how the plan is built

General principles (keep), plus choices for this user (fill in at setup).

## Principles
1. **Consistency beats intensity.** A plan the user actually follows beats a perfect one.
   Start easier than feels necessary; build ~5–10% per week.
2. **Easy days easy, hard days hard.** Most training (≈80%) is easy and conversational; at most
   2–3 hard sessions per week.
3. **A lighter week every 3–4 weeks** (≈60–70% of normal volume).
4. **Strength 2×/week** (even 20–30 min) protects joints and keeps muscle, especially in a
   calorie deficit.
5. **Minimum session rule:** on a bad day, 10–20 min (walk, mobility, easy spin) instead of
   zero, where safe. It keeps the habit alive.
6. **Recovery is training:** sleep, protein and one real rest day per week.
7. **Food:** protein at every meal; a deficit only in planned periods, never right before key
   events; eat enough around hard sessions.
8. **Pain:** traffic-light rule in `plans/injuries.md`. Swap, don't push through.
9. **Patterns over willpower:** if something keeps getting skipped, change the plan (time,
   place, prep), don't just "try harder".
